## HOW TO LIST ON ETSY (Step-by-Step)

### Step 1: Set Up Your Etsy Shop
1. Go to etsy.com/sell and create a seller account
2. Choose shop name, language, country, currency
3. Add billing info (credit card required)

### Step 2: For Each Product, Create a Listing

For each of the 10 themes below:

1. Go to Shop Manager > Listings > Add a listing
2. **Upload Images**: 
   - First image: Use `thumbnail_etsy.jpg` (the montage showing all 20 pages)
   - Additional images: Upload individual pages from `Original_Bilder/` (pick 5-8 best ones)
   - Etsy allows up to 10 images per listing
3. **Thumbnail video** (optional but recommended): Create a simple slideshow video from the PNGs

4. **Fill in Listing Details** (copy from `Etsy_Listing_Info.txt`):
   - Title (already SEO-optimized, max 140 chars)
   - Description (ready to paste)
   - Tags (13 tags, each max 20 chars)
   - Category: Paper > Digital Paper > Junk Journal Ephemera
   - Type: Digital Download
   - Price: $4.99 - $7.99 USD

5. **Upload Digital File**:
   - Attach `Product_Etsy.pdf` as the digital download file
   - OR list the 20 PNGs as individual downloadable files

6. **Shipping**: Select "No shipping needed - it's a digital item"

7. Click Publish!

### Step 3: Pricing Strategy
- Start at $4.99 for competitive positioning
- After 5+ reviews, consider raising to $6.99
- Offer bundle discounts: "Buy 3 Get 1 Free"
- Consider a "Complete Collection" bundle at $39.99

1. **Title keywords**: Front-load important keywords (Junk Journal, Ephemera, Printable)

3. **Renew listings** every 30 days for fresh ranking
4. **Add all 13 tags** for each listing - Etsy uses these for search
5. **Use Pinterest** to drive traffic - pin your product images
6. **Respond to reviews** quickly to build seller reputation
7. **Cross-reference** between listings: "Check out our other junk journal kits!"
